Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload notes?

Sign in to your account, click on the "Share Note" button in the navigation bar. Fill in the title, subject, tags, and select whether you want to write content in Markdown format or upload a file (PDF, TXT, doc) directly from your device.

Are these resources free?

Yes! NoteShare is built as an open peer-to-peer sharing catalog where students collaborate and support each other. All shared documents are free to download.

Can I edit or delete my notes?

Yes. If you log in and go to "My Uploads", you'll see a complete list of your shared documents. Opening any of your notes will show a red "Delete Note" button which you can use to remove your content instantly.
